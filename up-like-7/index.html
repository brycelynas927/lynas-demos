17+ Years Cutting

Clarke Road's Chair

Up Like 7 Barber Shop has been part of East London's community for over 17 years. Putta opened the shop on Clarke Rd because he believed the east end deserved a proper barbershop — not a franchise, not a walk-in chain, but a real chair with a real barber who takes time.

Every client — whether it's a first-time fade or a standing weekly appointment — gets the same level of attention. That's why people drive from across London just to sit in this chair.

Walk-ins are always welcome. But regulars know: book ahead and you're in and out looking sharp.
