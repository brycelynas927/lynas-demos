What We Do

Full electrical services
for homes & businesses.

⚡

Emergency Fault Repairs

Tripped breakers, no power, burning smells, or flickering lights — we treat electrical faults as the emergencies they are. Available 24/7 across London and Middlesex County. We'll diagnose and fix the issue fast, safely, and with a full explanation of what caused it.

Available 24/7
🏠

Residential Electrical

Everything your home needs — new builds, full house rewires, panel upgrades, additional outlets, kitchen and bathroom circuits, outdoor lighting, EV charger installation, and smoke detector upgrades. We handle it all from first consultation to final inspection.

🏢

Commercial & Industrial

Office fit-outs, retail lighting, three-phase power, data cabling, exit and emergency lighting, scheduled maintenance contracts, and tenant improvement work. We work around your business hours to keep disruption to a minimum.

🔌

Panel & Service Upgrades

Outdated 60-amp or 100-amp panel holding you back? We upgrade to 200-amp and beyond, add space for new circuits, and replace old fuse boxes with modern breaker panels. Required before selling many older London homes — we handle all ESA permits and inspections.

🚙

EV Charger Installation

Level 2 home charger supply and installation for all major EV brands. We assess your panel capacity, run a dedicated circuit, and install your charger to ESA code — everything you need to charge overnight at home. Permit and inspection included.

☀

Lighting Design & Retrofit

LED retrofit, pot light installation, under-cabinet lighting, dimmer systems, outdoor and landscape lighting. We design the layout, supply the fixtures, and handle the full installation — one trade, one invoice, zero hassle.

Who We Are

Built on honesty.
Measured in results.

Horizon Electrical was founded in 2009 by Dan Kowalski, a London-born electrician who wanted to build something different — a company where the quote you're given is the price you pay, where the crew shows up on time, and where every job gets a proper Certificate of Inspection without you having to ask for it.

Fifteen years later, we're still the same operation — just bigger. Our team of licensed electricians handles everything from emergency repairs to full commercial fit-outs, and we haven't changed the way we do business. You'll always know what you're paying, what we're doing, and why.
