Our Story

A Restaurant Built on One Idea.

Copper & Thyme started with a simple belief: the best meal you can eat in London, Ontario is one made from what's actually growing in Ontario right now. Not imported, not frozen — just what the farms around us have this week.

Emma opened the doors in Old South in 2019 with eleven tables, a wood-fired oven, and relationships with five local farms. Today those relationships have grown to eighteen producers across Middlesex County — and the menu still changes every time something better comes off the land.

Every dish traces back to a specific farm, a specific field, a specific harvest. We put that on the menu so you know exactly where your dinner came from.
