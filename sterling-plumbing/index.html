Who We Are

A Plumber Who Actually Picks Up.

I'm Dave. I started Sterling Plumbing & Drain in 2003 after a decade with larger outfits — watching homeowners get overcharged for simple jobs. The idea was simple: show up when you say you will, do honest work, and don't pad the invoice.
