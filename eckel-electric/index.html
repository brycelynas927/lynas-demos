What We Do

Our Electrical Services

From new builds to EV chargers to fire alarms — we handle the full scope of residential and commercial electrical work across Middlesex Centre, London, and St. Thomas.

🏗️

New Construction Wiring

Full electrical installation for new homes and commercial builds. We work with contractors and developers throughout Middlesex Centre to wire properties from the ground up — on schedule and up to code.

⬇️

Underground Wiring

Safe, code-compliant buried electrical runs for outbuildings, garages, shops, and outdoor structures. Properly rated conduit and cabling designed to hold up in the Ontario climate.

🔥

Fire Alarm Systems

Installation, wiring, and testing of fire alarm systems for residential and commercial properties. We ensure your system meets all Ontario fire code requirements and is properly certified.

🔌

EV Charging Stations

Get your home or business ready for electric vehicles. We install Level 1 and Level 2 EV chargers — fast, safe, and done to code so your charger runs reliably for years to come.

About Eckel Electric

Delaware's Own Electrician.

I'm Brad Eckel, owner of Eckel Electric. I started this business because I believe electrical work should be done right — safely, cleanly, and built to last. No shortcuts. No guessing.

Based in Delaware, Ontario, I serve homeowners, contractors, and businesses across Middlesex Centre, London, and St. Thomas. Whether you're building a new home, running power to an outbuilding, or setting up an EV charger — I'll get it done properly the first time.
