GOOD QUESTIONS

Let's clear things up.

Here's what you should know before purchasing a digital spreadsheet. We want you to know exactly what you're getting.

Is this a monthly subscription?

No. Each template has a one-time listed price. No ongoing template access fee is required.

What do I get with a template?

A downloadable ZIP containing a blank Excel workbook ready for your own data, a separate workbook with fictional examples, a quick-start PDF guide and a single-business-use license.

Can I use it in Google Sheets?

These products are designed as Excel .xlsx workbooks. We haven't verified full Google Sheets compatibility, and some Excel features may behave differently when imported.

Will it automate client bookings or send invoices?

No. These are manual-entry trackers with built-in spreadsheet calculations—not online booking platforms, invoice generators, payroll systems, tax software or payment processors.

Are the spreadsheet previews exact screenshots?

The graphics on this storefront are illustrative previews. The actual products are Excel files with their own dashboards, forms and spreadsheet tabs. You can review the sample workbook included after purchase.

When can I purchase?

The storefront catalog is available to explore. Checkout will open once the store's live payments and automatic digital delivery have been tested. Until then, purchase buttons are marked unavailable.
