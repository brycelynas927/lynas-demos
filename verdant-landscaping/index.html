Our Story

Mark Holloway

Owner & Operator, Verdant Landscaping

I grew up in Byron, cutting lawns around the neighbourhood for $20 a pop to save for my first car. I learned early that people genuinely appreciate it when their property looks cared for — and I've never stopped caring about that.

I started Verdant in 2012 with a used truck and a trailer I welded together myself. We're still based in London and we still do the same work — only now we've got a crew of four, a full kit of equipment, and 200+ properties across the city that trust us to show up every week and do the job right.

We're not the cheapest option in London, and we're not trying to be. What we are is reliable: we show up when we say we will, we do the work properly, and we don't disappear after the first season. If something isn't right, you call me directly and we fix it.

— Mark Holloway, Owner
