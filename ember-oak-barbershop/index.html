Our Story

Built on craft,
not shortcuts.

Ember & Oak was opened in 2019 by Marcus Reid, a barber with over twelve years of experience. After years cutting hair in Toronto and New York, Marcus came back to London to open the kind of barbershop he always wanted — one that takes the time to do it properly.

No rush. No compromises. Every client gets the same level of care whether it's their first visit or their fiftieth.
