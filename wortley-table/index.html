Our Story

Claire Moran

Owner & Founder

I grew up in Old South, about ten minutes from where this pub now stands. Moved to Toronto for a while — worked in restaurant kitchens there for most of my twenties, learned what good food service actually looks like — and came back to London because this city kept pulling at me. I opened The Wortley Table in 2021 because I wanted a place in this neighbourhood that felt like it belonged here. Not a chain. Not a concept. Just a room with good food and enough space at the bar to talk to someone you just met.

What we do differently is quieter than it sounds: we source from Ontario farms when the season allows it, we write a new menu section every few weeks, and we don't rush tables. The kitchen takes the food seriously. The front of house takes the guests seriously. That combination is rarer than it should be, and it's the only thing I ever wanted to get right.

If you're from Wortley Village or Old South, there's a decent chance I've already seen you in here. If you're coming from Byron or downtown for the first time, welcome — the patio fills up fast on Friday nights, so call ahead if you can. Either way, there's a table for you.

— Claire Moran, Owner
