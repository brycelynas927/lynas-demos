Our Story

A Barbershop Built for the Neighbourhood

Tom Callahan grew up getting his hair cut at a proper barbershop — the kind where you knew the barber's name and he knew yours. When the last old-school shop in Wortley Village closed in 2016, he decided to open one himself.

Clippers & Co. has been serving Wortley Village, Old South, and the surrounding neighbourhoods since 2017. No frills, no gimmicks — just sharp cuts, fair prices, and a place where you actually feel welcome. Walk-ins are always open, and appointments are easy to book.

Tom and his team live and work right here in London. This is their community too, and they treat every client like it.
